About Us

Our Story

itisus is a multifaceted company specializing in construction and digital solutions.

Under itisus built, we focus on residential buildings and medium-scale construction projects, delivering high-quality construction drawings and building designs.

itisus digital offers a range of creative design services, including report writing, documentation, advertisements, presentations, and logo design.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative solutions through Itisus Built's construction expertise and Itisus Digital's creative services, enhancing clients' physical and virtual presence while building lasting relationships.

Our Vision

To become the trusted partner for businesses and individuals seeking seamless integration of physical spaces and digital experiences, driving growth and success through our collaborative approach.

Our Core Values

  • Innovation: Embracing creativity in both construction and digital solutions.
  • Collaboration: Fostering strong relationships between Itisus Built, Itisus Digital, clients, and partners.
  • Quality: Delivering exceptional results in design, construction, and digital services.
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty, transparency, and ethical practices in all interactions.
  • Client-Centric: Prioritizing client needs, goals, and satisfaction in every project.
  • Growth: Encouraging learning, development, and forward-thinking in our team and industry.
  • Sustainability: Embracing eco-friendly practices and solutions.

Our Journey

  • 2023: itisus founded
  • 2023: Launched itisus Digital
  • 2023: Launched itisus Built
